BUILD YOUR OWN LEVEL

You describe the level. Claude builds it, and gives you a link anyone can play, with its own leaderboard.

1. CONNECT FRANK TO CLAUDE

  1. Open claude.ai → Settings → Connectors and choose Add custom connector.
  2. Name it Free Fall Frank and paste this address:
    https://freefallfrank.com/mcp
  3. Save, then Connect: you are asked to sign in with Google and allow it. That is what makes the levels and Franks you build yours, on any device.

No Google account? In the game, open settings (the sliders, top left) and PROFILE, and copy your connector address: the same address with your own code on the end. Add that instead and there is nothing to sign in to.

In Claude Code: claude mcp add --transport http frank https://freefallfrank.com/mcp

2. ASK FOR A LEVEL

In a new chat, with the connector switched on, say what you want. For example:

Make me a Free Fall Frank level called Lava Run: about a minute, starts easy, a maze in the middle and a brutal finish.

Build a level for my little sister: slow, lots of coins, in the candy world.

A level made only of patterns, left-left-right-right, getting faster each time.

Claude shows you what it will be like, changes it as you say, and publishes it when you are happy.

3. PLAY IT AND SHARE IT

You get a link like freefallfrank.com/?level=abc1234. Open it and the level is added to your levels. Send it to anyone: it is free, with nothing to install, and everybody's best score goes on that level's leaderboard.

The public list. To put a level in front of every player (PLAY THEIR LEVELS in the game), press GO PUBLIC beside it on your profile, or ask Claude to submit it. It is free; we look at each level before it is listed.

4. MAKE YOUR OWN FRANK

Ask Claude for a Frank too: a name, his colours, a hat and a few extras.

Make me a Frank called Pirate Frank: red shirt, black trousers, an eye patch and a cowboy hat.

You get a link; opening it adds him to your Franks. And you can ask Claude to change your name on the leaderboards or the Frank you play as.

WHAT A LEVEL CAN HAVE

Patterns: a gate every beat, with the taps you choose (left, right, double taps, rests).

Gates: count your taps, with posts, springs, blowers, saws and bonus rooms.

Slime: he slides instead of tumbling.

Mazes: paths that fork and join.

Drops: a fast fall to catch your breath.

And any of the 24 Franks' worlds for its look and its music. Every level can be finished: the game checks.

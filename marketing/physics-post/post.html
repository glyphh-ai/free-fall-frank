THE PHYSICS OF THE FALL
FRANK = 1×3 BLOCKS
GATE · ON THE BEAT
"1>" = ONE TAP, RIGHT
↓ rpb ROWS EVERY BEAT
1 · THE SONG IS THE CLOCK
beat = seconds × BPM ÷ 60
No gravity, no random. 100 BPM.
2 · WHERE FRANK IS
row = row₀ + (beat − b₀) × rpb
rpb = rows per beat, fixed per stretch, so every gate lands on a beat.
3 · HOW FAST HE FALLS
speed = rpb × BPM ÷ 60

4 · A TAP = A TUMBLE
↻90°
θ = ±90° × t ÷ 0.09s
x = px + dx·cosθ − dy·sinθ
y = dx·sinθ + dy·cosθ
He swings round his bottom corner (px): 1×3 becomes 3×1.
5 · THE FAN TURNS THE WORLD
a += (turn − a) × (1 − e−10·dt)
The board swings to 45° or 90°, shrinking to 80% at a quarter turn. Only the view turns: the rules stay put.

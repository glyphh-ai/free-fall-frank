HOW IT PLAYS

TWO THUMBS. ONE PROBLEM.

THE FALL

ONE LONG WAY DOWN

Elm Street. Down the manhole, under the city. It is one level, about three minutes from the street to the bottom at normal speed, with more than 200 gates on the way, and every gate lands on the beat of the music.

Each gate needs an exact number of taps one way. Then come the posts, the springs, the blowers, the swinging saws and the bonus rooms.

MAZES AND SLIME

THE PATHS FORK. PICK ONE. FAST.

Two stretches of the fall are mazes: double taps down paths that fork and join again, with a wall down each side. Either way round gets you through.

In the sewer it is too slimy to tumble, so Frank sits in his duck ring and every tap slides him one step across. Then the slide, where he falls twice as fast.

LEADERBOARDS

CAN YOU BEAT ME?

Every level has its own leaderboard, and it keeps each player's best. The further down you get and the more tokens you grab, the more you score; reaching the bottom is worth a big bonus, and hard and insane multiply the lot.

No account needed: you are on the board from your first fall, under a name you can change. Clear the level and the game makes a picture of your win to share, or press REC and film up to a minute of your fall.

FAQ

QUESTIONS

Is it free?

Yes. The game is free in your browser: the first level top to bottom, all four difficulties, the leaderboards, the levels players have made, and building your own. Three Franks are free from the start, and any other Frank can be earned for 250 of the tokens you pick up as you fall. There is nothing to install and no sign-up.

The second level, ROCK BOTTOM, costs $0.99. Its first stretches are free to try.

What does paying get me?

There are two things to buy, each once. One payment of $0.99 unlocks every Frank at once, for good: the 21 that are not free. A Frank is who you play as; the levels, the scores and the leaderboards are the same whichever Frank you are.

And $0.99 unlocks ROCK BOTTOM, the second level, for good: all of it, with its own leaderboard. Neither is a subscription.

Can I get a refund?

No. Both are one-time purchases and neither is refundable; the game says so before you pay. ROCK BOTTOM has a free taste, and any Frank can be earned with tokens, so you can see what you are buying first.

Phone or computer?

Both. It is made for a phone held upright: tap the left or the right half of the screen. On a computer use the left and right arrow keys, or A and D; Esc or P pauses. It runs in the browser, and you can add it to your phone's home screen.

How does the leaderboard work?

Every level has its own board, the levels players made included. Each attempt scores 100 points for every percent of the level you got through and 10 for every token, plus 5,000 for reaching the bottom; hard counts one and a half times and insane double. The board keeps each player's best. Press TOP on a level to see it.

You need no account to be on it. You go up as PLAYER and a number until you pick a name of 2 to 16 characters.

What are the difficulties?

Easy: a fall sends you back only to the start of the stretch, and tokens count half. Normal: a fall sends you back to the top. Hard: everything is a fifth faster, and scores count one and a half times. Insane: everything is 40% faster, and scores count double. You change it in the settings (the sliders, top left).

How do I make my own level with Claude, and share it?

In claude.ai open Settings, then Connectors, add a custom connector with the address https://freefallfrank.com/mcp, and connect it: you sign in with Google, which is what makes the levels yours. Then describe the level you want in a chat. Claude previews it, changes it as you say and publishes it.

You get a link like freefallfrank.com/?level=abc1234. Anyone who opens it plays your level, free, and their best score goes on its leaderboard. To put it on the public list in the game, press GO PUBLIC beside it on your profile or ask Claude to submit it; it is looked at before it is listed. All of this is free. The steps, one by one.

Do I need an account?

Not to play. Your tokens, your Franks, your scores and your levels are kept for your device. You sign in, with Google, only to pay, or if you want all of it on every device: signing in moves what the device has onto the account. You can sign out or delete the account from your profile in the game.
